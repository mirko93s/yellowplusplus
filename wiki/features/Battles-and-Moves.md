# Battles and Moves

## Battle rules

- **Physical/Special split:** moves are individually Physical, Special, or Status. Their category is no longer decided by type.
- **Separate Special Attack and Special Defense** replace the original single Special stat.
- **Modern critical-hit chances:** about 1 in 24 normally, 1 in 8 at +1 stage, 1 in 2 at +2, and guaranteed at +3. High-critical moves add one stage; Focus Energy and Dire Hit add two.
- **Sleep lasts 1–3 turns**, and Pokémon can act on the turn they wake up.
- **Burn, poison, and Leech Seed** deal 1/8 of maximum HP each turn instead of 1/16.
- Enemy stat-lowering moves no longer have an extra, artificial 25% chance to fail.
- Ultra Balls now have their own catch modifier.
- Both sides' battle stats are capped at **1,023**.

Updated type matchups:

- Bug against Poison: **half damage**, previously double.
- Poison against Bug: **normal damage**, previously double.
- Ghost against Psychic: **double damage**, previously no effect.
- Ice against Fire: **half damage**, previously normal.

These are selected updates from later games; Yellow++ does not use every rule from a single later generation.

## Battle screen and controls

- Redesigned battle HUD, including HP bars, level display, status symbols, and Poké Ball graphics.
- Colored icons show each move's **type and category**.
- An effectiveness indicator helps judge the highlighted move against the opponent. Sand Attack's indicator accounts for its ability to ignore type immunity.
- Hold **LEFT** in the move menu for power and accuracy; hold **RIGHT** for the move's effect description.
- Press **B** in a wild battle to quickly select **RUN**.
- Faster HP bars and smoother move-menu loading.
- Larger trainer prize-money amounts fit in the reward display.

## Updated move effects

- **Tri Attack** can burn, freeze, or paralyze, with about a **10%** overall chance.
- **Poison Sting and Sludge** have about a **30%** poisoning chance.
- **Acid, Aurora Beam, BubbleBeam, Bubble, Constrict, and Psychic** have about a **10%** chance for their stat-lowering effect.
- **Struggle** is typeless, with recoil changed to a quarter of maximum HP instead of half the damage dealt. Opponent recoil currently uses your Pokémon's maximum HP.
- **Pay Day** generates money equal to five times the user's level, up from twice the level.
- **Swift** no longer hits Pokémon during the invulnerable turn of Dig or Fly.
- Focus Energy, accuracy, healing, and other battle fixes are listed under [Bug fixes](https://github.com/mirko93s/yellowplusplus/wiki/Bug-Fixes).
