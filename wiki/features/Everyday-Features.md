# Everyday Features

## Getting around and saving

- **Running shoes:** hold **B** to run. Mother gives them to you in a new scene when you first leave your bedroom.
- **Always Run** is available in Options, so you do not have to keep holding B. With it enabled, hold **B to walk** instead; running still requires the running shoes.
- Interact with trees, water, and boulders to get **Cut, Surf, and Strength prompts**.
- Poison no longer damages Pokémon while walking.
- Pokémon Centers heal without an extra confirmation question.
- Trainers face you when they notice you.
- Saving has fewer prompts and less waiting, with a brief result display.
- The pause menu no longer has a separate EXIT entry.
- Maximum playtime is **99,999:59:59**.

## Bag and item shortcuts

- A graphical Bag with four pockets: **Items, Balls, Key Items, and TMs/HMs**.
- Room for **50 item stacks, 10 Ball stacks, 21 Key Items, and 55 TMs/HMs**. Ordinary stacks can hold **255** items.
- Item descriptions stay visible, with pocket-capacity and scrolling indicators.
- **SELECT** inside the Bag moves stacks and merges matching ones. Key Items and TMs/HMs use single entries.
- The player's item PC is removed. Its **Super Potion is now a hidden item at the bedroom PC**.
- The Master Ball is in the Balls pocket and cannot be sold or tossed.
- Register **one Key Item** for quick use with **SELECT in the overworld**. Open the item's action menu and choose **Select**; doing this again clears the shortcut. Choosing another item replaces it, and the assignment is saved.
- Supported shortcuts: **Bicycle, Itemfinder, Old Rod, Good Rod, Super Rod, Type Chart, Town Map, Coin Case, and Poké Flute**.
- The Bicycle opens a **Use / Select** menu. Choose Use to mount or dismount. Normal restrictions still apply to shortcuts, including where cycling or fishing is allowed.
- A **Repel renewal prompt** offers to use another of the same Repel when it wears off.
- Itemfinder turns you toward a detected hidden item.

## Type Chart

The Viridian school teacher gives you a reusable **Type Chart** after Grandpa's catching demonstration and school escort.

- Use it from the Key Items pocket or register it to SELECT.
- Shows all **15 types** and their single-type matchups on one screen.
- Read the attacking type on the **left**, and the defending type across the **top**.
- **Green:** super effective. **Red:** resisted. **Black:** immune. **White:** normal damage.
- Works with all four display modes, including readable monochrome labels.
- **A or B** closes it. It cannot be consumed, sold, tossed, or used in battle.

## TMs, supplies, and money

- **TMs are reusable**, with no remaining quantity displayed.
- Added **Zinc and X Special Defense** for the new Special Defense stat.
- Healing amounts: **Super Potion 60 HP, Hyper Potion 120 HP, Fresh Water 30 HP, Soda Pop 50 HP, Lemonade 70 HP**.
- Updated prices using Generation IX values where applicable. Items sell for **one quarter** of their purchase price, previously one half.
- Start with **5,000**, and carry up to **9,999,999**.
- Redesigned shops show item information and quantity selection. **LEFT/RIGHT** adjusts quantities by **10**.
- Celadon Mart 2F sells other supplies instead of TMs, including **Rare Candy and PP Up for 9,999 each**.

## Game Corner

- Buy **500 coins for 10,000**.
- Pokémon prizes are **level 25**: **Ditto 1,337; Pinsir 2,500; Scyther 2,500; Eevee 4,600; Dratini 6,500; Porygon 9,999** coins.
- TM prizes show move names: **Dragon Rage 3,300; Hyper Beam 5,500; Substitute 7,700** coins.

## Party, status, and move learning

- A **four-page, Generation II-inspired status screen** with natural stats (**DVs**), training stats (**EVs**), move types/categories, power, and accuracy.
- Switch pages and browse party members without closing the screen. Cries do not hold up navigation.
- Colored page markers and faster sprite loading.
- A redesigned move-learning screen shows **power, accuracy, and effect descriptions** before you replace a move.
- **SELECT** swaps Pokémon directly in the party list, replacing the separate SWITCH command.
- Party submenus wrap around and return to the party list when canceled.
- A **Move Relearner and Move Deleter** live in Saffron's Pidgey house. Relearning costs **1,000**; deletion is free and can remove HM moves.

## Pokédex

- Expanded entries show **base stats, types, evolution information, moves learned on evolution, and level-up moves**.
- Press **SELECT on any entry page** to preview normal or shiny colors.
- Height and weight use **meters and kilograms**.
- Updated entry layout and caught-Pokémon symbol, improved AREA information, and faster loading.

## Pokémon storage

- Bill's PC has a **Generation III-style graphical interface**, with Pokémon pictures and individual minisprites.
- Store **216 Pokémon in one collection**, browsed as **12 pages of 18 slots** in a **6 × 3 grid**.
- Manage the party alongside stored Pokémon.
- **SELECT** picks up a Pokémon to move it between pages or between storage and the party. Move onto an occupied slot to swap.
- Context menus provide summary, movement, release, and printing actions.
- Clearer selection, page navigation, cancellation, and faster loading.

## Character, text, and options

- Choose a **boy or girl**, with matching player graphics and animations, plus four suggested names.
- Expanded naming characters and automatic uppercase/lowercase switching after the first letter.
- Dialogue uses normal capitalization throughout the game.
- Faster text, with **FAST** selected by default; battle style defaults to **SET**.
- Choose between **two fonts**, original and NES-inspired, and **15 text-box frames**.
- Four color modes: **Uncorrected, GBC correction, DMG Green, and Black/White**.
- Options and Cheats support holding directions to scroll.

## Graphics, music, and presentation

- Replaced Pokémon front sprites and updated selected back sprites and colors.
- Unique Pokémon party minisprites, expanded overworld Pokémon graphics, and additional Gym Leader sprites.
- Separate overworld graphics for Bill, books, the Town Map, and the Pokédex.
- Updated player sprites, battle graphics, map tiles and colors, title screen, and Super Game Boy border.
- NPC colors are independent of the player's colors.
- A custom intro with revised music, Pokémon cries, and animation polish.
- Elite Four battles use the **Gym Leader battle theme**.
- The title/main menu includes a version identifier and a **GitHub QR code**.
- Smoother transitions and faster loading throughout the menus, Pokédex, status screen, and storage.
