# Modes and Cheats

## Game modes

Choose **Classic, Hard, Nuzlocke, or Hard + Nuzlocke** when starting a new game. Your Trainer Card shows the selected mode.

**Classic** includes the revised trainer teams and all the ordinary Yellow++ changes.

**Hard** adds progression-based level caps, forces **SET** battle style, and blocks item use in trainer battles. Its caps are **15, 26, 36, 46, 56, 61, 66, 70, and 85**, increasing to **100** after becoming Champion. Hard uses the same reworked trainer teams as Classic.

**Nuzlocke** limits catches by region, requires nicknames, and makes fainting permanent: ordinary healing and revival cannot bring fainted Pokémon back. Gifts also count against regional restrictions, so extra gifts available in Classic are subject to those limits.

The optional **Dupe Clause** checks whether you have caught a member of the same evolutionary family. Running from a duplicate preserves the area's encounter; defeating it still uses that encounter.

**Hard + Nuzlocke** combines both sets of restrictions.

## Seeded wild randomizer

- Optional during new-game setup, with a shareable text seed of up to **10 characters**.
- The same seed gives the same species mapping.
- Randomizes ordinary walking encounters, including **grass, caves, and surfing**.
- **Fishing, gifts, static encounters, and trainer teams** keep their own species.
- The Trainer Card shows randomizer and Dupe Clause information when enabled.

## Unlocking cheats

Enter **↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A** on the **main menu**, then start or continue with cheats enabled. The unlock and selected settings are saved.

Use **LEFT/RIGHT** to adjust settings and **B or START** to leave. Cheats are disabled during link activities.

## All cheat settings

These six settings can target **User, Foe, Both**, or be **Off**:

- **Infinite HP**
- **1-hit KO**
- **Always Crit**
- **Never Miss**
- **Super Eff.**
- **No Status** — prevents ordinary status conditions; does not cover confusion or Leech Seed.

Additional settings:

- **Infinite PP**
- **100% Catch**
- **Learn Any TM**
- **Infinite Items**
- **B: No Clip** — hold B to pass through overworld obstacles.
- **Infinite Safari**
- **All Shiny** — newly generated eligible Pokémon are shiny.
- **Perfect DVs** — newly generated eligible Pokémon have maximum natural stats.
- **No Wild Mons**
- **EXP Boost** — ×2, ×3, ×4, or ×8.
- **Money Boost** — ×2, ×3, ×4, or ×8 trainer rewards.
- **Wild Level** — set wild encounters to a level from **2–255**.
- **Next WildMon** — choose a species by Pokédex number **1–151**. The choice stays active until changed.

**All Shiny and Perfect DVs cannot be enabled together.** Wild overrides apply to walking and fishing encounters, rather than trainers or scripted battles. Catch and encounter cheats still respect checks such as Nuzlocke restrictions and available storage space.
